# Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

An Excel-based retail sales analytics project focused on analysing product performance, monitoring KPIs and identifying business opportunities through data visualisation.

## Dashboard Preview

![Retail Sales Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning and Validation
- SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, IF, MAX, MIN
- INDEX and MATCH
- KPI Reporting
- Data Visualisation
- Business Analysis

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | £6,620 |
| Total Units Sold | 55 |
| Top Revenue Product | Laptop |
| Top Product Revenue Share | 60.42% |

## Key Business Insights

1. **Revenue Concentration:** Laptop generated £4,000, representing 60.42% of total revenue despite selling only five units.
2. **Sales Volume:** Mouse recorded the highest sales volume at 20 units but contributed only 4.53% of revenue.
3. **Business Recommendation:** Investigate opportunities to grow revenue from other products and reduce dependence on Laptop sales.

## Project Deliverables

- Excel workbook containing sales analysis and dashboard
- Revenue by Product bar chart
- Revenue Contribution doughnut chart
- KPI summary and business recommendations

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates foundational Excel data analytics skills, from data preparation and KPI calculations to dashboard development and communicating business insights.
